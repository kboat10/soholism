# Ground rules

1. **Only attack what we own.** Our VMs, our routers, our test devices.
2. **Written permission first** for any business review: scope, dates, contact person, signed.
3. **Strictest law wins.** UK Computer Misuse Act 1990 and Ghana's Cybersecurity Act 2020 both apply to us as a team.
4. **Personal data stays out.** Strip names, phone numbers, MAC addresses of other people's devices and public IPs from logs and screenshots before committing.
5. **No secrets in Git.** Private keys and passwords live in a password manager, never in this repo.
6. **Disclose privately.** If we find a real vulnerability in someone's product, we report it to them first and wait before publishing.
