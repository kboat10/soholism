# SOHO Security Playbook

A plain-language guide for small offices and home businesses in Ghana and the UK. Written by the Two-Site SOHO team from what we built and broke. Licensed CC BY 4.0.

## Outline (fill in during weeks 7–8)

1. **Who this is for** — shops, clinics, small agencies, home businesses
2. **The 30-minute fixes** — router password, firmware updates, guest Wi-Fi, WPA3/WPA2
3. **Split your network** — staff, guests and smart devices kept apart, and why
4. **Block bad sites for free** — DNS filtering
5. **Connect two branches safely** — VPN basics
6. **Know when something's wrong** — what to log and watch
7. **Budget build** — what we spent, in cedis (GHS) and pounds (GBP)
8. **Top 10 mistakes we found** — from `attacks/`
9. **Checklist** — one printable page
